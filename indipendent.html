⚔️ The Journey

India's Freedom Struggle

Key moments that shaped India's path to independence

1857

The First War of Independence

Also known as the Sepoy Mutiny, this was the first major revolt against British East India Company's rule, led by soldiers across India.

1885

Indian National Congress Founded

The INC was established, providing a political platform for Indian freedom fighters to unite and demand self-governance.

1919

Jallianwala Bagh Massacre

British General Dyer ordered troops to fire on peaceful protesters in Amritsar, killing hundreds. This tragedy galvanized the freedom movement.

1920

Non-Cooperation Movement

Mahatma Gandhi launched a nationwide non-violent protest, urging Indians to boycott British goods, institutions, and courts.

1930

Salt March (Dandi March)

Gandhi walked 388 km from Sabarmati Ashram to Dandi to make salt, defying the British salt tax in a powerful act of civil disobedience.

1942

Quit India Movement

"Do or Die" — Gandhi's call to the British to leave India sparked a massive civil disobedience movement across the country.

1943

Azad Hind Fauj (INA)

Subhas Chandra Bose formed the Indian National Army to fight for India's independence alongside the Axis powers in WWII.

1947

🇮🇳 India Gains Independence

On August 15, 1947, India finally gained independence. Jawaharlal Nehru delivered his famous "Tryst with Destiny" speech at midnight.

Heroes of Independence

Brave souls who dedicated their lives for India's freedom

🕊️

Mahatma Gandhi

Father of the Nation

Led India to independence through non-violent civil disobedience, inspiring movements for civil rights across the world.

🌹

Jawaharlal Nehru

First Prime Minister

A central figure in the independence movement and architect of modern India as a sovereign democratic republic.

⚔️

Subhas Chandra Bose

Netaji

Formed the Indian National Army and gave the rallying cry "Give me blood, and I shall give you freedom!"

🔥

Bhagat Singh

Revolutionary Hero

One of the most influential revolutionaries who sacrificed his life at age 23 for India's independence.

👑

Rani Lakshmibai

Queen of Jhansi

Led the revolt of 1857 against the British, becoming a symbol of resistance and bravery for the nation.

📘

Dr. B.R. Ambedkar

Father of the Constitution

Architect of India's Constitution and champion of social justice, fighting against caste discrimination.

🦁

Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel

Iron Man of India

United India by integrating 562 princely states into the Indian Union, earning the title "Iron Man of India."

🌺

Sarojini Naidu

Nightingale of India

Poet, political activist, and the first woman governor of an Indian state, a key figure in the independence movement.
